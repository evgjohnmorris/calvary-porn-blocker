Ministry Setup & Compliance Guide

1. Accountability Partners

To comply with ministry standards, you must configure at least one accountability partner. Go to the Accountability tab, enter their email and phone number, and provide SMTP/Twilio credentials to enable automatic alerts for bypass attempts or lockdown changes.

2. Lockdown Mode

Enable Lockdown Mode from the Overview tab to prevent any unauthorized changes to filtering levels or VPN settings. This enforces a strict environment required for compliance.

3. Deep System Scanning

Use the System Scanner to periodically audit the device for explicit material or history. Quarantine or delete flagged files to maintain a pure environment.

4. Global Compliance Standards

To comply with international data protection and content regulation standards across all countries (e.g., GDPR in the EU, CCPA/COPPA in the US, LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada, APPI in Japan, and global ISO standards), ensure that all collected logs, alerts, and accountability data are handled with strict confidentiality. This system's lockdown and logging mechanisms are designed to align with universal legal frameworks for administrative tracking and internet safety.

ISO Compliance Note: This system enforces logging and alerting. All administrative actions are recorded in the Developer & Logs tab.
